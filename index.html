Biography

Tanaya Julian Suhairi (handle: tanayajs), known competitively in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) as 4DELL. and Pudell., is an Indonesian esports player, coach, and MPL media contributor based in Jakarta.

During high school at SMA Bunda Hati Kudus Jakarta, 4DELL. competed as the primary Goldlaner for the school's varsity roster, Aeterna Invictus, earning multiple inter-school podium finishes and achieving Top Global #6 X.Borg in MLBB Season 39. Following graduation and while pursuing higher education at Bina Nusantara University (Binus), Tanaya transitioned into Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Professional League (MPL) media coverage with GGFeed.id and returned to SMA Bunda Hati Kudus Jakarta as the school's official MLBB Esports Coach, leading the roster to championship titles in 2026.
